Privacy Policy for Bujji: Voice AI Companion

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

App Developer: Bujji Team (Emmadi Vashishta)

1. Overview

Bujji ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to empowering Indian elders to navigate their smartphones naturally through voice. We strictly believe in elder privacy, transparency, and data protection. We do NOT sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties or advertising brokers.

2. Permissions & Data We Access

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Bujji accesses your device contacts solely to allow voice-initiated phone calls (e.g. "Call Amma") and WhatsApp messaging to your family members. Contact details are stored securely on your local device / private backend and are NEVER uploaded to public directories or shared with third parties.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Bujji utilizes the camera exclusively when you choose to scan a physical medicine strip or bottle label using the Medicine Scanner. Captured images are analyzed in real-time by Google Gemini Vision to read medicine names and instructions, and are not retained or used for biometric tracking.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used for real-time speech recognition so elders can speak naturally in Tanglish, Telugu, or English. Audio is converted to text for intent processing and is never stored as audio recordings.
  • Notifications & Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver critical pill alarms and medication reminders directly to the elder's lock screen.

3. Artificial Intelligence & Processing

Bujji utilizes Google Gemini APIs to comprehend multi-turn conversational context in Indian languages. Queries sent to the AI processing layer are anonymized and contain only the necessary user context to answer questions, guide payments safely, or extract medication instructions.

4. Financial & Payment Safety

Bujji never asks for, stores, or transmits bank account numbers, credit card details, or UPI PINs. The UPI Guide provides educational voice instructions only and repeatedly warns elders never to disclose their UPI PIN to anyone.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Users may delete their profile, contacts sync, and reminder history at any time by tapping the "Reset" button within the application.
